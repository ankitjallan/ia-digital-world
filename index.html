Prompt library

Replace the facts with your client's, and verify every output.

1. Understand the business before the first voucher

You are a senior internal auditor (Chartered Accountant) in Guwahati.
Client: a private limited company that buys aluminium billets, extrudes sections, and sells
ACP panels, office partitions, sliding/casement windows and doors to dealers and builders
across Assam and the North East. Turnover approx Rs 60 crore. Accounting in Tally.

1. Explain the business cycle from billet purchase to installation in 8-10 steps.
2. For each step list the key internal-audit risks specific to THIS industry
   (e.g. yield/scrap, LME-linked pricing, site installation billing, dealer credit).
3. Give the 10 data fields I should request from Tally/ERP for each step.
Output as a table: Step | Risks | Data to request | Suggested analytics test.
If you are unsure about any industry norm, say so instead of guessing.

2. Risk-control matrix & audit programme in one pass

Using the risks you identified, prepare a Risk & Control Matrix for the
Procure-to-Pay cycle of the aluminium client.
Columns: Risk ID | Risk | Likelihood (H/M/L) | Impact (H/M/L) | Expected control |
Control type (preventive/detective, manual/automated) | Test of control |
Substantive / data-analytics test | Data source (Tally report / ERP table).

Then convert it into an internal audit programme aligned to ICAI Standards on
Internal Audit: objective, scope, procedures, sample/100% approach, evidence to
retain in working papers. Company policies: GM can approve payments up to
Rs 2 lakh, above that Director; vendor creation needs PAN + GSTIN + cancelled cheque.

3. Ask Claude to analyse the data you uploaded

Attached: Sales_Register and Customer_Master for FY 2025-26
(synthetic / masked). Company policy: maximum trade discount 5%;
office closed Sundays; financial year ends 31-Mar-2026.

Act as an internal auditor. Test 100% of invoices for:
a) discount above policy, and which salesperson gives it
b) invoices dated on Sundays
c) duplicates (same customer, date, item, qty, value)
d) gaps in invoice numbering
e) cut-off: invoiced in March but dispatched in April
f) inter-state supply charged CGST+SGST instead of IGST
Show each exception list as a table with invoice numbers, then a
summary of patterns. State exactly how you tested each one.

4. Policy / SOP gap review against best practice

Attached is the client's "Purchase & Vendor Management Policy" (8 pages).
1. Compare it with good practice for a manufacturing SME in India:
   vendor onboarding (PAN/GSTIN validation, bank verification, related-party
   declaration), segregation of duties, approval matrix, 3-way match,
   rate contracts for commodity inputs linked to market benchmarks.
2. List gaps as: Policy clause | Gap | Risk | Recommended wording.
3. Flag clauses that conflict with each other.
4. Draft 5 questions I should ask the purchase head in the walkthrough.
Quote the exact clause numbers from the document; do not paraphrase as if quoted.

5. Reading invoices, e-way bills and agreements

Attached are 12 scanned purchase invoices and e-way bills
for aluminium billets.
Extract into a table: Supplier | GSTIN | Invoice no | Date |
Item | Qty (kg) | Rate | Taxable | GST | E-way bill no |
Vehicle no | Distance.
Then flag: GSTIN format errors, invoice date after e-way bill
date, qty mismatch between invoice and e-way bill, rate
outside Rs 245-275/kg, the same vehicle used on the same day
for two far-apart deliveries.

6. Reading invoices, e-way bills and agreements

Attached: installation contract with a builder for
aluminium windows at a residential project (14 pages).
Summarise: scope, rates, escalation clause (LME-linked?),
milestone billing, retention money, defect liability period,
liquidated damages, payment terms, termination.
Then tell me which audit tests apply: unbilled milestones,
retention receivable ageing, LD exposure, revenue recognition
(Ind AS 115 / AS 7) issues.

7. From exception list to a well-written observation

Draft an internal audit observation in 5C format
(Condition, Criteria, Cause, Consequence, Corrective action)
plus Risk rating and Management response placeholder.

Facts: vendor V031 "Shree Ganesh Metal Pvt. Ltd." was created
on 18-07-2025 by user PUR_BIKASH with the same PAN, GSTIN and
bank account as existing vendor V001 "Shri Ganesh Metals Pvt Ltd".
Two advances of Rs 3.5 lakh and Rs 4.1 lakh were paid to V031
with no invoice. Vendor creation SOP requires accounts approval.

Tone: factual, non-accusatory, suitable for the audit committee.
Do not conclude fraud; recommend investigation steps.
